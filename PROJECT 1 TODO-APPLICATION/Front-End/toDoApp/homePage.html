About ToDoApp

ToDoApp is your personal productivity companion. With user-specific dashboards, you can manage tasks effortlessly:

  • 📝 Add and manage tasks in seconds
  • ⏰ Set due dates and get visual alerts for overdue or soon-to-be-due tasks
  • 📊 Track created and updated times for better accountability
  • ✅ Mark tasks as completed with a single click
  • 🔎 Search and filter tasks by keywords
  • ⚠️ Stay on top of deadlines with color-coded notifications
